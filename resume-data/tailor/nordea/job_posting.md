## About the Role

Central role in shaping Nordea's credit risk modelling landscape. Join the IRB modelling functions to drive design, enhancement, and implementation of credit risk models across the organisation. Combines advanced modelling, practical IT implementation, and sound banking understanding.

## About the Team

Risk Models team — develops and maintains models under IRB, IFRS9 and Credit Risk Stress Testing. Accurately estimates IRB risk parameters for Nordea's customers across multiple countries.

Collaboration. Ownership. Passion. Courage.

## Main Responsibilities

- Develop technical tools for model development and analysis using Python
- Work on model lifecycle activities: data pipeline, data quality, development, documentation, implementation, maintenance, monitoring, requirement setting, user acceptance tests within credit and impairment models
- Contribute to methodological standards, modelling tools, coding practices, and continuous improvement of the modelling framework
- Drive projects within IRB Models; manage stakeholder relationships in Risk, IT and Business
- Present & justify analyses, results, and implications to stakeholders (periodic and ad-hoc)
- Provide technical guidance, review work of other modelers, support modelling strategy and priorities
- Stay informed on regulatory developments and industry practices
